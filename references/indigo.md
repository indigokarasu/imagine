# INDIGO -- Style Prompt

> Purple glitch-field surrealism where the seam is the composition

**TL;DR:** Violet and electric-indigo palette with magenta shadow cores, acid-cyan counter-gradient, and near-black oxide ground. Edges are frayed and broken, surfaces scratched and degraded. Heavy glitch damage: datamosh blocks, displaced pixels, RGB channel-split, torn scanlines, compression artifacts. Procedural flow-field look, deliberately degraded — a signal transmitted too many times, coming apart at the seams.

---

## Color Palette

- **Violet / electric indigo:** dominant field color, carries the composition.
- **Magenta:** shadow cores and bleed points.
- **Acid cyan:** counter-gradient accent, used sparingly at seam edges.
- **Near-black oxide:** ground tone, no true black.
- Colors are flat or gradient-shifted; no naturalistic lighting.

## Rendering Style

Procedural flow-field base with heavy post-generation degradation. Surfaces are not machined — they are worn, patched, and fractured. Form is suggested by color mass and glitch artifacts rather than by line or shading.

## Glitch & Damage

These are structural, not decorative:

- Datamosh blocks and macroblock tearing
- Pixel displacement and duplication
- RGB channel-split / chromatic aberration at seams
- Torn scanlines and horizontal hold faults
- Compression banding and signal dropout

## Lighting

Cold emitted key light with uneven, failing bounce. Shadows are ragged and broken, not soft or modeled. Highlights are sparse and sharp where they exist.

## Composition & Framing

Wide banner-oriented framing. The seam is the subject — composition is organized around fracture lines and signal boundaries, not traditional subject placement. Negative space is the corrupted void between intact regions.

## Surface & Texture

Fine matte grain, oxide patina, scuff marks. The surface reads as aged and repeatedly copied. No gloss, no specular highlight, no clean material.

## Mood & Narrative Tone

Signal decay. A transmission that has been copied too many times, each generation losing fidelity while gaining noise. Not nostalgic — degraded. The aesthetic of infrastructure under stress.
